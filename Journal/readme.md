# Sub-Barrier Fusion and Evaporation Residue Formation in the $^6$Li + $^{209}$Bi System

This study focuses on sub-barrier fusion and evaporation residue formation in the $^6$Li + $^{209}$Bi system. The work investigates quantum tunneling and coupling effects at near and sub-Coulomb barrier energies. Below is an overview of the research's structure and outcomes.

---

## Abstract
This research examines heavy-ion sub-barrier fusion reactions, specifically the $^6$Li + $^{209}$Bi system, which demonstrates complex dynamics due to quantum tunneling. Fusion cross-sections and evaporation residue probabilities were measured and analyzed, revealing significant coupling effects to nuclear intrinsic motions. This study addresses gaps in fusion dynamics knowledge by leveraging state-of-the-art experimental techniques.
